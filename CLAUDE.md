Business rules should be changed deliberately, with concrete examples and behavior tests. This revamp preserves inherited tariff values; do not accidentally change them while working on infrastructure or layout.

Use fictional fixtures for tests. Preserve working `.data` state, browser recovery copies and pending revisions during maintenance. Run `pnpm verify` before shipping a change.
